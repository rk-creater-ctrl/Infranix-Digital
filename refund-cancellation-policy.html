Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds are handled for custom digital services.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Custom Digital Services

Infranix Digital provides custom websites, software, automation, and related digital services. Each project is discussed and scoped directly before work begins; this website does not offer automated online checkout.

Before Work Begins

If a project is cancelled before work starts, any payment already received will be reviewed based on the agreed scope and payment terms. Where no work or reserved third-party cost has been incurred, a refund may be available.

After Work Has Started

Payments for completed work, time already spent, approved milestones, third-party purchases, and non-recoverable costs are not refundable. If a project is stopped after work begins, the remaining balance and any applicable refund are assessed against the work completed and the agreed terms.

Scope Changes and Delays

Requests that materially change the agreed scope may require a revised quote or timeline. Delays caused by missing client content, approvals, access, or feedback do not automatically create a refund right.

Cancellation Requests

Please send cancellation or refund requests in writing with the project name and reason for the request. Infranix Digital will review the request and respond with the applicable outcome.
