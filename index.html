Answers About Our Engineering & Solutions

Common questions from enterprise leaders, institutions, and businesses partnering with Infranix Digital.

What services does Infranix Digital specialize in?

Infranix Digital engineers enterprise digital solutions across seven core domains: Web Development, School & Institutional Management ERP Software, AI Chatbots & Automation, Custom Python Software, Business Workflow Automation, Robotics & IoT Embedded Systems, and Proactive Website Maintenance.

What is the EDU-NOVA School Management ERP?

EDU-NOVA is our comprehensive institutional school management system designed for schools, academies, and colleges. It manages student admissions, fee tracking, biometric & face attendance, marksheet generation, parent notifications, and teacher administration with multi-role access controls.

Can Infranix Digital build custom AI chatbots for our business?

Yes. We build intelligent, custom-trained AI chatbots and conversational assistants for 24/7 customer support, instant FAQ responses, lead qualification, and WhatsApp or CRM integrations, trained specifically on your company's proprietary services and knowledge base.

Can we hire Infranix Digital for custom Python software & automation?

Absolutely. We build custom Python desktop software, automated billing engines, report and certificate generators, web scrapers, data pipelines, and administrative automation tools that streamline business operations and eliminate manual repetitive work.

Do you provide full source code and post-launch maintenance?

Yes. Upon completion and delivery, clients receive full ownership of the software application and codebase. We also offer dedicated monthly SLA maintenance packages covering server uptime, security patches, backups, and feature improvements.
